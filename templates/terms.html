1. Description of Service

Cheztrap is a scam detection tool that helps users judge whether a website or Gmail email is safe. When you click the extension, it checks the page or email you are viewing: its address, visible content, sender, sender verification results, links, and attachments. It combines our own automated checks, public domain registration records, and AI analysis (provided by OpenAI) to give a verdict, a short explanation, and the evidence behind it. Background details such as a company's founders, headquarters, traffic, or funding are AI-generated estimates and may be incomplete or out of date.
